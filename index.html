a little about me

I’m a Frontend Developer in progress (HTML & CSS), currently exploring UX/UI while I build my technical foundation step by step. I like creating clean, functional and visually pleasing interfaces — ideally with minimal panic involved, but we work with what we have.

I come from Customer Experience, Business Analysis, public relations and fashion production, which basically means I’m very familiar with people, patterns, chaos, and pretending everything is under control while fixing things in real time.

Right now I’m learning JavaScript and building small projects as I go. I Google a lot, I experiment even more, and somehow things start making sense eventually. My approach is simple: stay curious, stay consistent, and trust the process (even when the process is a bit delulu).

My goal is to grow into a solid frontend + UX/UI profile, where strategy, design and empathy actually coexist — and where I can build things that not only work, but also feel good to use.
